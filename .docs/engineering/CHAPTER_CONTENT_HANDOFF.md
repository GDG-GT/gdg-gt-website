membership contact, learning resources, team, gallery, sponsorships, GDG-GT

account. Links and ordering remain editable in the normal Linktree workspace.

The application has four sections and 13 configured fields, plus the native
required name/email identity fields. It collects study details, up to three
contribution interests, motivation, an idea, experience, availability, an optional
portfolio link, an optional 10 MiB PDF and accuracy/recruitment consent. It has a
final answer review, resumable/editable responses and 180-day retention. Applicants
may request an exact questions-and-answers copy at only the email they enter;
file names are listed rather than attaching private uploaded bytes. This option
is separate from the verified-identity administrative receipt workflow.

Access currently accepts entered identity as the owner requested; it does not
claim an entered email is verified. SSO/Google remains deferred. One-response
mode is intentionally off until a verified identity/invitation flow is selected.
No deadline, specific vacancy or recruitment decision was invented. The form is
Open; officers can close it, add a schedule or revise positions and publish a new
version through Forms. Existing submissions retain their published version.
